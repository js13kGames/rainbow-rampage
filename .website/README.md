You are Roy G. Biv, magical Unicorn. But your world's colors have faded! Someone is taking them all and it is solely up to you to take them power!

Navigate through multiple levels, collecting Rainbow Colors, draining box color power, and defeating enemies to gain back all the colors of the Rainbow!

As you collect more colors and restore your world to its former state, you will notice the world become colorful again all around you!

Move - WASD or Arrow Keys or Left Stick or D Pad

Aim - Mouse or Right Stick

Shoot - Left Click or Right Trigger

Reload Weapon - R or Right Click or Right Top Bumper

Select Color Power - Q or Tab or [ and ] or Left and Right on D Pad

Use Selected Color Power - E or Left Face Button

Hug closely to colored boxes to gain their power, but watch out because the box will fade away! Also watch out for shooting any boxes as they are apt to disappear!
